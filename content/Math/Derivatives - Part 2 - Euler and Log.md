# Meaning of the Exponential (e) or the Euler’s number


1. We see the exponential e or the [[Euler]] number** appear a lot of times in different fields. Its numerical value is ~2.71. One way to think of it is in the equation below. As we increase the value of n, the equation converges to 2.71. **Another property of e is that it is its own [[derivatives]]**. That is the reason we see it so often everywhere.

## The derivative of e^x
1. Let’s look at the function y = e^x. Say we want to calculate the slope at x = 2. Say we approximate the slope using delta x as 1 to begin with and keep reducing the intervals, we will notice that the slope approaches 7.39 which is precisely the value of the function at that x = 2. This happens for every point and thus e^x is a derivative of itself.
