- The question then arises how many type 1 errors are you willing to let go?

- This is where the #significance_level  comes into play. Below both extremes are bad. We need balance.

![[image 6 7.png|image 6.png]]

![[image 7 7.png|image 7.png]]

- To give the proper definition of significance level, it is the **maximum probability of committing a type I error**, which is the same as the maximum probability of rejecting H0 when H0 was actually true. The value of alpha is your design criterion for this test. This means that alpha will determine a threshold to decide if you should reject H0 or not based on your sample.
